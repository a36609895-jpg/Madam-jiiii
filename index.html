It’s not just about your looks. Mujhe tera character, tera style, tera way of talking, teri little habits, everything feels special. I don’t know how to explain it, but there’s something about you that makes me smile without any reason.

Honestly, I don’t just like looking at you. I like the person you are. And if I ever get the chance, I’d love to make more memories with you, spend time with you and just live those little moments together.

I don’t know what the future holds, but one thing I know is that I genuinely love the feeling of having you around.
